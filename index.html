About

I am an incoming PhD student at the University of Cambridge, starting in Autumn 2026, where I will be advised by Martin Kleppmann. I also collaborate actively with Joseph Bonneau and Benedikt Bünz. My research broadly focuses on building secure systems using modern cryptographic techniques, with a particular emphasis on SNARKs. You can find my publications, talks and service on the research page.
